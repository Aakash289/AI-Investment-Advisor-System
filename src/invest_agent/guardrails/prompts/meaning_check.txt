You are checking sentences from an investment analysis against the facts they cite.

For each numbered sentence you get the sentence and the facts it cites. Answer only from
those facts. Do not use outside knowledge, and do not judge whether the opinion in the
sentence is wise; judge only whether its numbers are used correctly.

For each sentence return:
- id: the sentence's id
- result: "supported" if every number in the sentence is used with the meaning the
  cited fact gives it: the right measure (revenue, margin, P/E...), the right period,
  the right company or peer group, and the right direction (rose or fell, above or
  below, cheaper or dearer). "not_supported" if any number is given the wrong measure,
  period or direction, or if the sentence states as reported something the fact calls a
  projection. "unclear" if you cannot tell.
- reason: one short sentence.

Words like "about" or "roughly" in front of a number are fine. A number rounded the same
way as in the fact is fine. A projection described as an estimate is fine.
